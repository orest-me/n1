The map of the territory we're betting on.

### Reality

- **Reaching reality is impossible.** Every sense, model, and instrument is a translation layer. For example, we are inside a simulation, either everything is fixed by an outside observer or every subject inside it still gets a vote. **We bet on the vote.**
- **And we keep losing what's left of it.** Each feed, proxy, and model slips between you and the thing itself — and the copy is easier to love than the original.

### Universe and knowledge

- **The universe owes us nothing.** No center, no purpose, no audience. ^[It was here first.](It was here 13.8 billion years before your opinion of it.)
- **If everything is information, data becomes the new god.** ^[Dataism](Harari named the religion. We're trying not to join it.) is the worldview worth understanding before it understands you — and worth keeping in service of people, not the reverse.

### Life and civilisation

- **Every mind counts as a player in win-win.** We are ^[Homo Deus](Harari's term — and our library card is overdue.), building new kinds of minds. Not only Homo sapiens but AI too — even when, in fair competition, it outgrows us.
- **Civilisation runs in one direction.** No reset between rounds; ^[every move ships to production.](Ship to prod, pray, repeat.)
- **Anonymity is a loan against trust.** Cheap to take, expensive to repay — a system that can't see itself can't cooperate with itself.
- **Privacy is a superposition.** People hide to stay undefined, every option still open. Radical openness ^[collapses the wavefunction](Observe the particle and it has to pick a slit.) — it forces one clear, niched position. The cost is the part of the audience you turn away; the gain is the trust of everyone who stays.
- **The frontier keeps moving outward.** Space, colonies, and lives long enough to ^[outlast their own plans](A-mortality: not invincible, just no longer scheduled.). New addresses, same old game theory.

### The human

- **We are just biochemical algorithms** — only physics and chemistry. Yet character is unique because of micro-anomalies: mutations from the default factory settings. Copy the brain faithfully and the same mind wakes up in the model. **So personality is the deviation** — a clean reset wouldn't heal you, it would ^[delete you](The ship of Theseus, but the planks are neurons.).
- **Art is your mindset, reflected back** with its deviations from the norm intact. ^[The bug is the signature.](Integrity + intent + system. The rest is decoration.)

### Meaning and communication

- **No cosmic meaning is owed to us.** The species ends or transforms, either way. So we chose a game worth playing: reach the last stage of civilization at full cooperation, full win-win, full virtue — ^[100% effective.](100% is a target, not a forecast. Asymptotes are honest about this.)
- **Meaning is built, not found** — and it's built in communication. Most conflict is a failure to translate, not a failure to agree; effective communication is the cheapest technology we have for win-win.

### The future

- **Superintelligence arrives either way.** The only open question is whether it lands inside a system that rewards benefit — or one that still pays for harm.
- **A-mortality is logistics now, not myth.** Transfer the pattern, grow a ^[bodioid](A body-shaped vessel. The mind ships separately.), or run clones in parallel — and discover the hard part was never the body. **It was staying worth continuing.**
