:::callout
**Data collection + Proactivity + Data openness = Result.**
:::

Three moves. Each is useless alone and compounding together.

1. **Collect data.** Internal and external. Find the patterns, build the models, run the forecasts — you cannot manage what you refuse to measure.
2. **Be proactive.** Close vulnerabilities before they cost you. Build ^[real strength](Strength you can audit. The other kind is called a press release.), not the illusion of it. Always hunt for the win-win.
3. **Open the data.** Transparent intentions and actions build trust — and trust is the cheap entry into win-win. A real ^[public commitment](Cortés burned his ships. We just publish ours.) makes defection too expensive and virtue maximally profitable.
