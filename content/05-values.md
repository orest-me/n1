title: Values
---
1. **Win-win or no deal.** Every interaction either creates mutual value or doesn't happen.
2. **Truth.** Accurate models over comfortable narratives — starting with self-deception.
3. **Rationality.** Evidence and logic over consensus and emotion — including the obligation to update when proven wrong.
4. **Transparency.** Default to open. Remove information gaps, starting with your own.
5. **Wisdom.** Knowing which game you're playing, and why it's worth playing.
6. **Genius.** The unfair solution — maximum effect from minimum, surprising means.
7. **Obsession.** The mission is not a hobby. It is the thing you cannot put down.
8. **Discipline.** Do the boring thing on the boring day. Compounding requires showing up.
9. **^[Temperance.](Aristotle's golden mean — itself a kind of ratio. We approve.)** Strength held in reserve. The opposite of impulse.
10. **Awareness.** See the system you're inside — including yourself inside it.
11. **^[Antifragility.](Taleb's term. He'd want us to note he invented it. We just did.)** Build mechanisms that get stronger when attacked.
12. **Leverage.** Minimum force at the point of maximum systemic effect.
13. **Courage.** Act on the Mission despite resistance from those who profit from the status quo.
14. **^[Skin in the Game.](Also Taleb. He's doing well in this section.)** Live the system yourself. The founder is the first evidence.
15. **Patience.** Compound interest mindset — urgency in action, patience in expectation.
16. **^[Humor.](To understand the universe is clever. To find it funny is wisdom.)** Only the free can laugh at what they cannot escape.
