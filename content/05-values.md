title: Values
---

1. **Win-win or no deal.** Every interaction either creates mutual value or doesn't happen.
2. **Truth.** See reality as it is, not as you wish it were — starting with your own self-deception.
3. **Wisdom.** Knowing which game you're playing, and why it's worth playing.
4. **Leverage.** Minimum force at the point of maximum systemic effect.
5. **Obsession.** The Mission is not a hobby. It is the thing you cannot put down.
6. **Courage.** Act on the Mission despite resistance from those who profit from the status quo.
7. **Transparency.** Default to open. Remove information gaps, starting with your own.
8. **Humor.** Don't take the world around you too seriously — humor makes life far more interesting.
9. **Discipline.** Do the boring thing on the boring day. Consistency beats intensity.
10. **Patience.** Compound interest mindset — urgency in action, patience in expectation.
11. **Rationality.** Evidence and logic over consensus and emotion — including the obligation to update when proven wrong.
